Oceania

People Culture

Oceania, a vibrant region cradled by the vast Pacific Ocean, offers a kaleidoscope of cultural experiences as diverse as its islands. From the ancient traditions of the Maori in New Zealand and the Aboriginal cultures in Australia to the colorful festivals of Polynesia, the region's heritage is a living tapestry of rituals, art, and storytelling.

Learn a bit more about New Zealand’s indigenous heritage and history while embracing some of the customs of the Maori tribe. Discover inspiring cultural tours at Te Puia. Join a Yura Aboriginal guide as you explore an 800-million-year-old landscape. Discover ancient rock engravings in a sacred canyon, listen to creation stories over a glowing sunset or seek out some of the oldest fossils on Earth. Wandering through the world’s oldest living rainforest along secret tracks and culturally significant sites, and throwing an insight into the Kuku Yalanji way of life. This is undoubtedly the true Mossman Gorge experience. Discover The Highlands Of Papua New Guinea - Life in the Western Highlands.

Oceania’s unique blend of ancient customs and contemporary influences creates a captivating cultural journey, inviting visitors to explore its majestic landscapes and vibrant communities. Dive into this mosaic of traditions and let the spirit of Oceania enrich your soul.

Wildlife & Nature

The fauna of Australia, New Zealand, and the Pacific Islands epitomize exclusivity, found solely within these distinctive regions. Here, the focus is on natural splendor and awe-inspiring landscapes, which serve as habitats to approximately 375 mammal species, around 1000 avian species, close to 1000 reptilian species, fewer than 250 amphibian species, and between 4000 to 5000 species of fish.

Explore the vibrant hues of the Great Barrier Reef, witness the breathtaking spectacle of a sunset at Uluru, embark on a heli-hiking adventure across a glistening glacier, seek out elusive kiwis, get up close to the kangaroos and cuddly koalas. Snorkel or dive with manta rays in the South Pacific islands. Swim with whales, see large fruit bats, and spot a variety of coral, marvel at the kaleidoscope of tropical fish, and birds in a Polynesian country. Be amazed with the exotic reefs of the Solomon Sea with their colorful marine life, the highlands of Mount Hagen, and the unique biodiversity of Varirata National Park in Papa Guinea.

All of these extraordinary experiences are facilitated by accommodations tailored to your preferences or by designing an itinerary by one of the top travel agencies in India that mirrors your unique style.

Culinary Escape

Oceania offers a culinary journey that celebrate the region's rich flavors. In Australia, savor the innovative fusion of indigenous ingredients and modern techniques at top-tier restaurants. New Zealand's farm-to-table dining showcases the freshest local produce, accompanied by world-class wines. The Pacific Islands invite you to indulge in traditional feasts, where you can taste authentic dishes prepared with age-old methods.

Dine in one of the South Pacific’s most romantic locations on a candlelit floating pontoon anchored in the magical calm of the Blue Lagoon. Take part in an authentic aboriginal dining and learn about the methods that the local Anangu people have used to hunt and gather food, as well as a chance to taste some of the local delicacies. Savor the scenic vistas from New Zealand’s premier whisky hut perched on a mountain saddle at 6,800 feet. Sip wine with a personal touch in South Australia. A famed Barossa Valley wine estate lets you sip vintage tawny from the year of your birth straight from the barrel on a Taste your Birth Year experience. Satisfy crayfish cravings on the Kaikōura coast or go winery hopping in Waiheke Island.

Each gastronomic adventure in Oceania offers an unforgettable blend of local ingredients, cultural heritage, and culinary artistry

Art Design

Oceania, characterized by its multifaceted cultural mosaic and stunning landscapes, presents a plethora of art and music encounters that enthrall the senses and pay homage to the region's abundant heritage. From the dynamic street art adorning Australia's metropolitan hubs to the time-honored chants resonating from Pacific Island nations, Oceania's artistic milieu is both varied and captivating.

From uncovering the stories, history and magic of the Sydney Opera House with visit areas off-limits to the public and capture photographs from rare vantage points. To cities like Melbourne and Sydney are adorned with colorful street art, where laneways and buildings become vibrant canvases for local and international artists. Learn the Maori Artistry renowned for its intricate detail, profound symbolism and deeply rooted in the spiritual beliefs, legends, and values of the indigenous Maori people. Immerse themselves in the rich Maori art experience through visits to cultural centers, museums, and art galleries across New Zealand. Or choose to explore The Pacific Islands are a treasure trove of traditional art and music, where ancient practices are preserved and celebrated. Tapa cloth making, wood carving, and intricate tattooing are among the traditional crafts that continue to thrive in countries like Fiji, Samoa, and Tonga.

From the urban streets of Australia to the remote villages of the Pacific Islands, Oceania's creative spirit invites travelers to immerse themselves in a world of artistry, storytelling, and cultural richness.
